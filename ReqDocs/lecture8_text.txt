Pseudorandom Number Generation Based on an Asymmetric Cipher
PRNG  based  on RSA:
???Known  as the Micali???Schnorr  PRNG .
???This PRNG  has much  the same  structure  as the output  feedback  (OFB)  mode  
used  as a PRNG .
???The encryption  algorithm  is RSA rather  than  a symmetric  block  cipher .
???A portion  of the output  is fed back  to the next  iteration  of the encryption  
algorithm  and the remainder  of the output  is used  as pseudorandom  bits.
???The motivation  for this separation  of the output  into two distinct  parts  is so that the 
pseudorandom  bits from  one stage  do not provide  input  to the next  stage .
